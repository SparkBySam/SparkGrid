What works

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Spark Grid 1.1.1

You have a workbook already. Here is what Spark Grid does with it, and what it doesn’t do yet.

Files

  • .xlsx opens and saves. So does CSV.
  • New, Open, Save, Save As, and recent files. Autosave writes a file you have already saved.
  • Open from the Open dialog, or drop a file on the app icon.
  • Several sheets: add, rename, delete, and switch. Drag a tab to move it. Tabs keep the file’s order, and a new order saves.
  • Defined names in an Excel file are read. Names you create are saved.
  • Column widths and row heights come back the same after you save and reopen.
  • Formulas Excel saved as blank stay blank, instead of showing 0.

Formulas

A formula cell shows its result. The formula stays in the formula bar. These 52 functions calculate:

  • Math: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, COUNTBLANK, MIN, MAX, ABS, ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, SUMPRODUCT
  • Conditional totals: SUMIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIF, AVERAGEIFS. Criteria can use wildcards.
  • Logic: IF, IFS, IFERROR, IFNA, AND, OR, NOT, TRUE
  • Lookup: XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH, XMATCH, COLUMN
  • Text: CONCAT, CONCATENATE, TEXTJOIN, TEXT, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, LEN, TRIM, UPPER, LOWER, SUBSTITUTE, CHAR
  • Dates: DATE, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, TODAY, NOW

Any other function shows #NAME?. Missing a function you use? Tell Sam which one.

Formatting

  • Fonts, colors, borders, and alignment.
  • Number formats keep the file’s format code: thousands separators, the currency symbol, and the date and time pattern.
  • Conditional formatting: highlights, color scales, data bars, and three icon sets.
  • Formatting comes back the same when you save and reopen, including in files with conditional formatting.
  • Merged cells. Titles draw across the merge at full size.
  • Freeze panes. Frozen rows and columns stay put when you scroll.

Working in the sheet

  • Selection, fill handle, zoom, and resizing rows and columns.
  • Filter any column from its header: search its values, see how many rows hold each one, and sort A to Z or Z to A. Click the same sort again to put the rows back. Undo works on filters.
  • Copy, cut, and delete skip rows a filter hides, as in Excel.
  • Find and replace on the current sheet.
  • ⌘-arrow jumps to the edge of your data. Add Shift to select as you go.
  • ⌘-click or ⌘-drag removes cells, rows, or columns from a selection.
  • Pictures you can move and resize.
  • Bar, line, and area charts on the sheet, with series color, point colors, title, legend, axis titles, and gridlines.
  • Print preview for the selection, the used range, the sheet, or the workbook.

Big workbooks

  • Large files open fast. A 48,000-formula workbook went from about 15 seconds to about half a second in 1.1.
  • An edit on the sheet you’re looking at shows up near-instantly. Other sheets catch up in the background.
  • Saving doesn’t freeze the window. Switching sheets is immediate.
  • Scrolling back to a page you’ve seen is instant. The first visit to a far part of a very large sheet can still hitch.

Not there yet

  • Spark Grid is not Excel or Numbers. No pivot tables, data-validation lists, comments, or live collaboration.
  • No pie or scatter charts, and no charts with more than one series.
  • The fill handle copies. It doesn’t continue 1, 2, 3 or a run of dates.
  • Filters pick from a list of values. They don’t filter by greater than, less than, or text contains yet.
  • The name box shows the address. It doesn’t take an address or a name. No F2 to edit.
  • Pressing Space in Finder doesn’t preview a workbook in Spark Grid.

Fixed in 1.1.1

These matched Excel only partly in 1.1.

  • ROUND on an exact half cent now rounds the way Excel does, so 1463.095 gives 1463.10.
  • Newer functions saved by Excel (XLOOKUP, XMATCH, IFS, TEXTJOIN, and CONCAT) calculate instead of showing blank.
  • INDEX across one row, like INDEX(B1:M1, 10), gives the tenth cell.
  • Formulas with brackets keep their brackets when you sort, fill, or paste them, so (1-F3) stays grouped.

Privacy

No account. No analytics. The app doesn’t use the network, and your files stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
