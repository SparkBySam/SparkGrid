A native Mac spreadsheet for .xlsx and CSV

Spreadsheets. No account.

Big workbooks open faster. The sheet you’re editing stays quick. No account, and your files stay on your computer.

Spark Grid 1.1.1 is out on the Mac App Store. It runs on macOS 14 or later. What this version includes.

macOS 14 or later · Built by Sam Parker · Part of the Spark Suite

Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV

For a workbook you already have. Spark Grid 1.1 is the version on the Mac App Store.

  • .xlsx and CSV Open and save both on your Mac. Autosave writes a file you have already saved. What’s in 1.1.
  • Everyday formulas SUM, IF, SUMIF, XLOOKUP, and VLOOKUP, plus text and date functions. The 1.1 list. Any other name shows #NAME?.
  • Filters and formatting Freeze panes, merge, filters, sort, find and replace, conditional formatting, pictures, and bar, line, and area charts on the sheet.

More than one sheet

Add, rename, and switch sheets. Freeze panes stay put, and a chart can sit on the sheet.

Formula cells show their results

The result is in the cell. The formula stays in the formula bar.

What’s new in 1.1.1

1.1.1 is the current release. Changelog.

  • A filter panel on every column: search its values, see how many rows hold each one, and sort A to Z or Z to A. Click the same sort again to put the rows back.
  • The tab bar shows how many rows a filter is showing, with one click to clear it. Filter changes can be undone.
  • Drag a sheet tab to move it. The new order saves with the file.
  • ⌘-arrow jumps to the edge of your data. Scroll bars appear while you scroll.
  • ⌘-click or ⌘-drag removes cells, rows, or columns from a selection.
  • Formatting comes back when you reopen a file that has conditional formatting, and formulas keep their brackets when you sort, fill, or paste.
  • Undo works after you open a file, and saving after a sort is quick.

Coming next

Not in 1.1.1, which is out now. What 1.1.1 includes.

Next up: nothing in a file gets lost when you save. Hidden rows, columns, and sheets stay hidden, and protection, dropdown lists, comments, links, tables, charts, and pivot tables stay in the file. After that: dropdowns you can pick from, a fill that continues numbers and dates, sorting by more than one column, and new Sort, Format Cells, Conditional Formatting, Insert Chart, and Print windows.
