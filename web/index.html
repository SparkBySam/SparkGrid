Spark Grid

A native Mac spreadsheet for .xlsx and CSV

Spreadsheets. No account.

Big workbooks open faster. The sheet you’re editing stays quick. No account, and your files stay on your computer.

Spark Grid 1.2 is out on the Mac App Store. It runs on macOS 14 or later. What this version includes.

macOS 14 or later · Built by Sam Parker · Part of the Spark Suite

Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV

For a workbook you already have. Spark Grid 1.1 is the version on the Mac App Store.

  • .xlsx and CSV Open and save both on your Mac. Autosave writes a file you have already saved. What’s in 1.2.
  • Everyday formulas SUM, IF, SUMIF, XLOOKUP, and VLOOKUP, plus text and date functions. The 1.1 list. Any other name shows #NAME?.
  • Filters and formatting Freeze panes, merge, filters, sort, find and replace, conditional formatting, pictures, and bar, line, and area charts on the sheet.

More than one sheet

Add, rename, and switch sheets. Freeze panes stay put, and a chart can sit on the sheet.

Formula cells show their results

The result is in the cell. The formula stays in the formula bar.

What’s new in 1.2

1.2 is the current release. Changelog.

  • Saving keeps everything a workbook had: hidden rows, columns, and sheets, grouped rows, tab colors, protection, page setup, dropdown lists, comments, links, tables, charts, and pivot tables.
  • Dropdown lists: pick a value from a cell’s list, or make your own with Data > Dropdown List.
  • Comments: rest the pointer on a cell to read its comment. Add and edit them from the cell’s menu.
  • Hide and unhide rows, columns, and sheets.
  • Filter by rule: contains, begins with, greater than, between, top and bottom, and dates.
  • The fill handle continues numbers, dates, and month and day names. The name box takes a cell or a name, and F2 edits the selected cell.
  • 37 more functions, including MAXIFS and MINIFS.
