Spark Grid

A native Mac spreadsheet for .xlsx and CSV

Spreadsheets. No account.

Big workbooks open faster. The sheet you’re editing stays quick. No account, and your files stay on your computer.

Spark Grid 1.3 is out on the Mac App Store. It runs on macOS 14 or later. What this version includes.

macOS 14 or later · Built by Sam Parker · Part of the Spark Suite

Open, edit, and save .xlsx and CSV

For a workbook you already have. Spark Grid 1.1 is the version on the Mac App Store.

  • .xlsx and CSV Open and save both on your Mac. Autosave writes a file you have already saved. What’s in 1.3.
  • Everyday formulas SUM, IF, SUMIF, XLOOKUP, and VLOOKUP, plus text and date functions. The 1.1 list. Any other name shows #NAME?.
  • Filters and formatting Freeze panes, merge, filters, sort, find and replace, conditional formatting, pictures, and bar, line, and area charts on the sheet.

More than one sheet

Add, rename, and switch sheets. Freeze panes stay put, and a chart can sit on the sheet.

Formula cells show their results

The result is in the cell. The formula stays in the formula bar.

What’s new in 1.3

1.3 is the current release. Changelog.

  • Charts saved in your workbooks appear on the sheet and update as you edit the cells they read.
  • Move and resize any chart, or edit its type, titles, legend, and series. Insert Chart makes pie and scatter charts, and charts with more than one series.
  • Find Duplicates: choose which columns must match, then highlight the repeated rows or remove the extras.
  • Tables with a styled header and banded rows. Tables in your files show their style.
  • Sheet tabs show their color. AutoSum adds a total with ⇧⌘T, and the sum, average, and count of a selection show at the bottom right.
  • A new Settings window where you can change any shortcut.
