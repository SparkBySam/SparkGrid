# Spark Grid 1.3 release notes

Drafts for App Store Connect. Review before pasting. Text for the App Store must not name other companies' products.

## What's New in This Version

Charts from your files now show on the sheet, and you can move, resize and edit them. Plus duplicates, tables, tab colors and a new Settings window.

New
• Charts saved in your workbooks appear on the sheet and update as you edit the cells they read. Rest the pointer on a chart to see its values.
• Move and resize any chart, or right-click it to edit, duplicate, bring to front or delete. Edit Chart changes the type, titles, legend and each series' cells and color.
• Insert Chart makes pie and scatter charts, and charts with more than one series.
• The Charts panel lists every chart on the sheet. Click one to go to it.
• Find Duplicates: choose which columns must match, see the repeated rows, then highlight them or remove the extras. Highlights are temporary and clear with Esc.
• Tables: Insert > Table turns a block of cells into a table with a styled header and banded rows. Change its style or remove it from the cell's menu. Tables in your files show their style.
• The sum, average and count of the selected cells show at the bottom right. AutoSum adds a total with Shift-Command-T.
• Icon sets can use your own thresholds, as numbers, percents or percentiles, and run in reverse.
• Sheet tabs show their color, and you can set one from the tab's menu.

Improved
• Settings has tabs for General, Shortcuts and About. Change any shortcut, search them, and see when one is already in use. Autosave can be turned off or set to save less often.
• Inserting a row or column on a large workbook is much faster.
• Comments are marked with a small speech bubble.
• The arrows that mark hidden rows and columns are easier to see.

Fixed
• Workbooks saved with a chart now open cleanly in other spreadsheet apps.
• Highlighting rules show in other spreadsheet apps after you save.
• Workbooks keep all their highlighting rules when they open.
• Closing Settings closes only Settings.
• A row keeps its gridlines after AutoSum.
• Icon set checkmarks are the right way up.
• Horizontal bar charts and area charts draw correctly.
